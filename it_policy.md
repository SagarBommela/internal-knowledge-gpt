# Artscape IT Policy Document

## Purpose

This document contains the official Artscape IT policies covering passwords, company devices, software installation, VPN and remote access, and data backup.

The IT Helpdesk Assistant must use this document as the authoritative source for policy-related questions.

---

## Password Policy

* Passwords must be at least 12 characters, including one number and one special character.
* Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
* Passwords cannot be reused for the last 5 changes.
* Account lockout occurs after 5 failed login attempts.
* A locked account requires IT to unlock it.

---

## Device Policy

* Company laptops are provided to full-time employees.
* Contractors must use personal devices with VPN access.
* All company devices must have antivirus enabled before first use.
* All company devices must have disk encryption enabled before first use.
* Lost or stolen devices must be reported to [it-support@artscape.com](mailto:it-support@artscape.com) within 24 hours.

---

## Software Installation Policy

* Employees may install approved software from the internal Software Catalog without additional approval.
* Any software outside the Software Catalog requires manager and IT approval before installation.
* Personal or non-work software installation on company devices is not permitted.

---

## VPN and Remote Access Policy

* VPN access is mandatory when connecting to internal systems from outside the office network.
* VPN credentials are separate from email login credentials.
* VPN access must be requested through the IT portal.
* VPN sessions automatically disconnect after 8 hours of inactivity.

---

## Data Backup Policy

* Company laptops back up automatically to OneDrive every 24 hours when connected to Wi-Fi.
* Employees are responsible for ensuring OneDrive synchronization is active.
* IT does not maintain separate manual backups.

---

## Knowledge Boundary

This document does not provide troubleshooting procedures for:

* Office Wi-Fi problems
* Broken laptop hardware
* Custom software bugs
* Network outages
* Other technical issues not explicitly documented in this policy

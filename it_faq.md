# Artscape IT Helpdesk — FAQ and Process Guide

## Purpose

This document contains documented IT helpdesk procedures and frequently asked questions for Artscape employees.

The IT Helpdesk Assistant must not extend these procedures with undocumented troubleshooting steps.

---

## How do I reset my password?

1. Go to `portal.artscape.internal/reset`.
2. Enter your work email.
3. Follow the link sent to your email to set a new password.
4. If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, contact [it-support@artscape.com](mailto:it-support@artscape.com).

**Source:** IT FAQ — How do I reset my password?

---

## How do I request VPN access?

1. Log in to the IT Service Portal.
2. Click **New Request** → **VPN Access**.
3. Your manager will receive an approval request.
4. Once approved, you will receive VPN credentials within 1 business day.

**Source:** IT FAQ — How do I request VPN access?

---

## How do I report a lost or stolen device?

1. Immediately email [it-support@artscape.com](mailto:it-support@artscape.com).
2. Use the subject line **URGENT: Lost/Stolen Device**.
3. Include the device serial number if known.
4. The serial number can be found on the bottom of the laptop.
5. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device within 1 hour of the report.

**Source:** IT FAQ — How do I report a lost or stolen device?

---

## How do I request new software?

1. First check the Software Catalog on the IT Portal.
2. Most common tools are pre-approved.
3. If the software is not listed, submit a **Software Request** ticket.
4. Include a business justification.
5. Approval typically takes 2–3 business days.

**Source:** IT FAQ — How do I request new software?

---

## Who do I contact for hardware issues?

For hardware issues such as a laptop not turning on or a broken screen:

* Email [it-support@artscape.com](mailto:it-support@artscape.com).
* Include a description of the issue.
* Include your asset tag number.

For urgent hardware failures:

* Walk-in support is available at the IT desk.
* Location: 3rd floor.
* Hours: 10 AM–4 PM on weekdays.

**Important:** This document provides reporting and support-contact information only. It does not provide hardware repair or troubleshooting instructions.

**Source:** IT FAQ — Hardware Issues

---

## Knowledge Boundary

This FAQ does not contain troubleshooting instructions for:

* Wi-Fi problems
* Network outages
* Broken laptop repair
* Custom software bugs
* Other technical problems not explicitly documented above
